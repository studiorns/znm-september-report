Executive Summary

DCT's Always-On programme generated 51.03M impressions, 189 publications and $1.3M in EMV around Zayed National Museum in September, led by the Creators Immersion Group visit. The dedicated Back to School campaign (2M impressions, 56 publications) is largely part of the same activity: 51 of the 62 posts in its monitor also appear in the Always-On export, so the two can't be added together. Its 0.10% interaction rate is a format effect: 56 of its 62 posts were Stories, and its Reels and TikToks engaged at 2.16% calc. Always-On's results rest on a few creators: two of 49 delivered half the impressions and 92% of the interactions export. Their followers were mostly in India and the US, with only 4.2% in the UAE calc.

Programme Deep Dives

Dedicated: Back to School

September supported the Zayed National Museum Back to School programme through family-led storytelling. Three family profiles were onboarded to show the museum's educational offering and family activities through real visits. The month's 56 publications also include content still pending from earlier visits and bonus content from previously contracted influencers. In the extra-budget monitor, 56 of the 62 posts were Instagram Stories, with 4 Reels and 2 TikToks; 51 of the posts also appear in the Always-On export.

DCT Always-On

Zayed National Museum gained strong visibility through DCT Abu Dhabi's Always-On programme and dedicated creator activations. The highlight was the Creators Immersion Group event: 50 local and international influencers visited the museum, followed by a hosted dinner. Combined activity generated 189 publications and 51.03M impressions.

49 creators posted in September export. The biggest posts were a Reel from @soyabeancoupleofficial on 20 September (5.75M impressions; recorded twice in the export, also at 5.10M) and Priya and Sid's YouTube Shorts and Reels on 20–23 September (2.4M–5.1M each).

Key Insights

Always-On carries the reach

51.03M impressions from 189 publications. Most Back to School posts are counted within it.

Group immersion works

Hosting 50 creators at once, with a dinner, produced the month's biggest wave of content and engagement.

Dedicated content is focused, not big

Family storytelling reached 2M impressions with a clear message, but each post reached far fewer people.

Dedicated engagement is a format effect

56 of 62 posts were Stories. The 6 Reels and TikToks engaged at 2.16%, close to Always-On's 3.05% on measured posts.

Video carries engagement and value

Reels and Shorts were 10% of Always-On posts but delivered 83% of interactions and 68% of EMV.

Two creators, half the reach

Priya and Sid and @soyabeancoupleofficial delivered 50% of impressions, 75% of EMV and 92% of interactions. Without Priya and Sid, September would have had about 37.7M impressions.

UAE creators: volume without reach

19 UAE creators made 91 posts (48%) but got 11% of impressions and 0.4% of interactions, averaging 62K impressions a post against 1.13M for US creators.

Audience mostly outside the GCC

Weighted by September impressions, the creators' followers were 30.0% in India and 25.6% in the US, but 4.2% in the UAE and 6.1% across the GCC.

Opportunities

Repeat the immersion event

Plan group creator visits around key museum moments, such as new exhibitions or programmes, to recreate September's spike.

New suggestion
Brief Always-On on museum priorities

Always-On delivers the reach. Giving its creators Zayed National Museum messages, such as Back to School and workshops, puts that reach behind current priorities.

New suggestion
Reuse the best family content

Use the strongest Back to School posts in owned and paid channels, where the story can reach beyond the creators' own audiences.

New suggestion
Set engagement benchmarks

Check each dedicated creator's typical engagement rate before contracting, so family profiles bring an engaged audience as well as a fit.

New suggestion
Report cost per result

With fees added, influencer cost per thousand impressions and per interaction can be compared directly with paid media.

New suggestion
Fix one pull date

Pull deck and export figures on the same date, for example the 5th of the next month, so reported and exported numbers match.

New suggestion
Ask international creators for video

Most large US and Indian creators posted stories only. Asking for at least one Reel or Short each would add measured reach and engagement.

New suggestion
Move UAE creators towards Reels

Use local creators for Reels and Arabic-language storytelling, as the dedicated campaign did, rather than story volume.

New suggestion
Clean the export before reporting

Remove duplicate post URLs, and use interactions ÷ impressions rather than Campaygn's ER, which reaches 219.6% on one post.

New suggestion
Add GCC creators

Only 4.2% of September's creator followers were in the UAE and 0.9% in Saudi Arabia. If domestic and regional visitors are the goal, add UAE, Saudi and other GCC creators with audiences in those markets.

New suggestion
Test the strongest measured performers

Ask @jounes.amiri17 (6.32%), @graceshadrack (6.19%) and @sachinsamson (5.20%) for more video. Each had one measured post in September, so their rates need more posts to confirm.

New suggestion
Widen the creator bench

Add more mid-to-large international profiles to each event, so a single creator doesn't account for half the month's results.

Things to Watch

  • How interactions are counted. Campaygn records no interactions for stories, so any programme's interaction rate depends on its share of stories. Compare formats, not programme totals.
  • Estimated story figures. Every story's impressions are set at 20% of the creator's followers, its reach at 75% of impressions and its EMV at $0.01 per impression. Stories are 52% of September's Always-On impressions.
  • Reliance on one creator. Priya and Sid delivered 30% of September impressions and 51% of EMV. Next month's figures will swing on whether creators of that size take part.
  • How EMV is calculated. The method isn't stated, and EMV is a modelled value, not money spent or earned.
  • Carry-over content. The dedicated figures include pending content from earlier visits and bonus content, so September looks bigger than the month's own activity.
  • Figures keep growing. Reels and Shorts gather views after the month ends. The 20 October export is already 6% above the slide's impressions.
  • One-off events. The immersion event made September exceptional. October's Always-On figures may fall back without a similar event.
