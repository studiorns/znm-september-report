Executive Summary

September delivered 36.3M impressions, 172.6K clicks and 2,545 purchases on $139.4K. Search was both the biggest and the cheapest converter, delivering 62.5% of purchases on 40.1% of spend at about $35 each. Meta was a reliable second at $42.38. LinkedIn spent $9.9K for no purchases, and Hero Display's $18K has no purchase measure, so it can only be judged on clicks.

Channel Efficiency

Cheap reach and cheap purchases are different things. TikTok and X buy attention for cents, but their purchases cost $115 and $276. Search costs the most per click among converting channels and still delivers the cheapest purchases, because 3.9% of its clicks convert, about 3.3 times Meta's rate.

Search is excluded from the CPM chart: it is bought per click, so its CPM (about $129.53) isn't comparable with display and social.

TikTok: Evergreen Awareness

19.9M impressions reached 3.8M people at a $0.53 CPM and $0.04 per view, with 10.7K landing page views and 92 purchases. Emirati Women Police and Astrolabe were the most efficient creatives at $0.49 CPM. 932.1K impressions and 9,023 clicks at a 0.97% CTR and a flat $2.00 CPC. Full Screen 1080x1920 clicked at more than twice the average, but on only 42K impressions.

Key Insights

Search is the conversion engine

62.5% of purchases on 40.1% of spend, at $35.09 each. It is the biggest and the cheapest converter.

Meta does the social converting

826 purchases at $42.38, plus 134 of the 148 membership purchases. Workshops is its strongest product at $39.30.

Cheap reach is not cheap buying

TikTok's $0.53 CPM and X's $1.71 CPM become $115 and $276 per purchase. Judge each channel on the job it was bought for.

LinkedIn earns attention, not purchases

A strong 32.34% video view rate but no purchases from $9.9K, the highest CPC of any channel ($3.02).

Opportunities

Shift budget toward Search

Prioritise English Brand and Generic, and keep Competition as an extra source of purchases. Check first that volume can grow, since brand search depends on demand.

Recommended + analysis
Bring Arabic search CPC down

Arabic Brand engages at 8.38% CTR but costs $3.44 per click. Refining terms, bids and keywords toward the English level is the cheapest efficiency gain available.

Existing recommendation
Lean on Meta statics for membership

Post 4 sold 50 and Post 5 converted at $29.38. Supply every ad in feed and story sizes, and move post engagement ads into awareness.

Existing recommendation
Test TikTok lower in the funnel

The awareness audience is already buying (92 purchases). A mid- and lower-funnel test would show whether its $115 cost per purchase can come down.

Existing recommendation

Things to Watch

  • X needs a full month. It returned after almost a year, so September is a first data point. So far it is the weakest channel that produced purchases.
  • LinkedIn has been tested with one ad only. One creative in one month isn't a verdict on the platform, but the next test should have a spend cap.
  • Hero Display has no purchase evidence. At $19.36 it has the highest CPM of the display and social channels and a $2.00 CPC. Without a conversion measure it can't be compared with the rest.
  • Search scale depends on brand demand. English Brand is the best performer, but brand search can only grow as fast as people search for the brand.
  • Small samples. Full Screen display (42K impressions), X's single membership creative and LinkedIn's single ad are too small to draw firm conclusions from.
  • Attribution isn't stated. It isn't stated how purchases are attributed on each platform. Search and social likely overlap, so cost-per-purchase comparisons are approximate.
