Social media monthly report, September 2026 (Sprinklr and native platforms) | Figures marked calc are calculated from the deck's figures

Executive Summary

The Museum gained 6,290 followers (+131% on August) and 25,902 interactions (+42%) from 150 pieces. The headline 0.18% engagement rate is a TikTok effect: TikTok, which includes paid activity, made 98% of impressions. Without it, the organic channels engage at 2.39%, inside the 1–5% benchmark. LinkedIn and Instagram drove growth, and Instagram is where the conversation happens.

Key Insights

Organic channels are healthy

Without TikTok, the five organic channels engage at 2.39%, 13 times the headline rate.

Growth is concentrated

LinkedIn, Instagram and TikTok made 91% of new followers.

LinkedIn's 5.20% is lifted by paid

Paid interactions divided by organic impressions. Without the paid Member September Animation, the rate is about 1.6%, below Instagram's 2.18%.

TikTok reach rests on paid

98% of impressions from 7 pieces. The top video was a paid creative and made 40% of all interactions.

Carousels underperform

Weakest post on five of six platforms. The same Member September content worked as video.

Low-return channels

Facebook, X and YouTube made 48 of 150 pieces (32%) and 235 interactions (about 1%).

Data Inconsistencies

The deck's arithmetic is correct: platforms add up to every total, and every engagement rate recalculates. These points concern what the figures include.

LinkedIn mixes organic impressions with paid views and interactions

Rate overstated
Impressions29,798 in the deck against 737,776 paid: paid excluded
Video views273,704 in the deck against 238,628 paid: paid included (about 35.1K left)
Top postMember September Animation, the paid creative: 1,075 of 1,550 interactions, about 50% of an average post's ~2.1K organic impressions
Sprinklr appears to separate LinkedIn impressions but not the boosted post's views and interactions, so the 5.20% rate divides paid-inclusive interactions by organic impressions. Without that post, LinkedIn engages at about 1.6% calc. Label LinkedIn as partly paid, as the deck does for TikTok.

TikTok "impressions" are views, and can't be compared with paid

Different measures

The decks use views in one and ad impressions in the other, so the figures can't be added or compared.
