September 2026 Website Analysis

Zayed National Museum website · Traffic, Campaigns & Bookings

GA4 property ZNM- GA4, 1–30 September 2026 | Bookings are sessions that reached the booking confirmation page | Figures marked calc are calculated

Executive Summary

The website had 219.3K sessions from 175.5K users in September, 69% from paid channels. 3,348 sessions reached booking confirmation, a 1.53% booking rate. Paid campaigns tripled daily traffic from mid-September, but paid social visits were short and rarely booked. Half of all bookings are credited to Direct, the ticketing site or "(not set)", so GA4 sees only 47% of the purchases the ad platforms report.

Daily Pattern

Daily traffic roughly tripled from mid-September as Paid Other (9 Sept), Paid Social (11 Sept) and extra Search budget started. It peaked on 19 September. The Always-On influencer posts on 20–23 September left no visible trace in Organic Social.

Session starts per day, which run about 1.3× GA4's session count (see Data Inconsistencies), so read the shape, not the level. Paid Other is Promofix, AOD and X.

Key Insights

Search brings buyers

Google Ads made the most bookings of any paid source (596), and Google organic books at the site average with no media cost.

Meta is closer to Search than it looks

On GA4's last-click numbers, Meta costs $72 per booking against Search's $94, the reverse of the platforms' $42 against $35.

Paid social visits are short

Meta, X and LinkedIn brought nearly 72K sessions at 14–23% engagement and about 30 seconds per visit. LinkedIn made 2 bookings.

Attribution leaks

49% of bookings sit in Direct, checkout-lane.com or "(not set)", and GA4 sees only 47% of platform-reported purchases.

AI search is already a channel

ChatGPT sent 563 sessions that booked at 3.0%, twice the site average.

Engagement isn't intent

Visit Abu Dhabi visitors had the highest engagement (65.7%) but booked at 0.19%. Judge sources on bookings, not engagement.

Opportunities

Exclude the ticketing domain

Add checkout-lane.com and any payment gateway to GA4's referral exclusions, and keep UTMs through the booking flow. This would move much of the 49% of leaked bookings back to their sources.

Highest priority
Track bookings with revenue

Mark the confirmation page, or a purchase event with value and ticket count, as the main key event so every report shows bookings and revenue.

Measurement
Track influencer links where used

It isn't known whether creators include links. Where they do (link stickers, bios or descriptions), suggest each uses a tracked link: utm_medium=influencer, utm_campaign by programme and month, and utm_content as the creator handle.

Measurement
Fix UTM conventions

Use paid-social for paid TikTok, and x or twitter as X's source, so both count as Paid Social. Stop "+"-encoding and retire old links.

Measurement
Fix ticket-click and duplicate tracking

Find why ticket_button_click fires 13–17 times per user for Direct and Referral, what changed on 18 September, and whether GA4 loads twice.

Measurement
Reconcile with agencies

Search (37% of platform purchases visible, more sessions than clicks), Epsilon (9K clicks, no tracked sessions) and Hero 1 (159 bookings, no spend reported).

Media
Report both cost views

Show platform cost per purchase and GA4 cost per booking side by side until attribution is fixed.

Media
Review LinkedIn and X

LinkedIn cost $4,976 per GA4 booking. X cost $243–276 per booking on either view and lost 57% of clicks before the site loaded.

Media
Build for AI and partner traffic

Keep key visitor information easy for AI assistants to cite, and add clear booking prompts on pages Visit Abu Dhabi links to.

Content

Things to Watch

  • What a booking session is. Bookings assume the segment counts sessions that viewed the confirmation page. A session can include more than one booking, and sessions aren't tickets or revenue.
  • Key events aren't bookings. The 3,348 booking sessions contain only 262 key events. Key events are a general engagement signal only.
  • Last-click credit. GA4 credits the final source before booking. Search and social likely play larger roles earlier in the journey than their last-click bookings show.
  • Landing pages and countries. The exports cover only 16% and 13% of sessions, so these cuts are left out until full exports are available.
